- **61% crave intense emotion. Who's delivering?**

- **70% can't remember being excited by a brand.**

- **Trust is scarce. Indifference is becoming expensive.**

- **Apathy may be marketing's greatest creation.**

The most dangerous thing a consumer can say about your brand isn't "I hate it." It's "I feel nothing." No outrage. No affection. No memory. Just another product, another post, another transaction.

Welcome to the real threat facing brands in 2027: emotional apathy.

VML's Age of Re-enchantment research found that 61% of consumers want brands to make them feel intense emotions, yet 70% cannot remember the last time a brand did anything that excited them.

We've never produced more content. Have we ever created less feeling?

## When Everything Becomes Forgettable

Imagine a world where every service is frictionless, every feed is personalised and every product performs exactly as promised. Convenient? Absolutely. Memorable? Not necessarily.

When nothing moves us, everything becomes replaceable. Brand loyalty gives way to price comparisons. Preference becomes a swipe. Culture becomes wallpaper.

## Is It Apathy, or Emotional Exhaustion?

But is the next generation apathetic, or simply exhausted by manufactured emotion? Algorithm-friendly excitement. Scripted authenticity. Influencer intimacy that ends at checkout.

Mintel's 2027 forecasts point towards authority shifts and a relevance reset. Consumers are questioning who deserves their attention, trust and emotional investment. Perhaps consumers haven't lost their capacity to feel. Brands have mistaken stimulation for meaning.

## The New Competitive Advantage: Making People Feel Something

The way forward is the move from brand experiences to emotional connection. Some brands are already showing what emotional relevance looks like in practice.

- Aesop transforms fragrance discovery into sensory intimacy.
- Patagonia turns sustainability into emotional commitment through repair.
- Nike builds belonging through shared running experiences.

Different categories. Same principle: emotion is earned through participation, not announced in a campaign.

As Gartner analyst Amy Abatangle observed, "Now, trust is scarce."

## Why Emotional Relevance Is a Business Imperative

For CMOs, this demands a harder measure of culture marketing, experiential marketing and brand storytelling. Beyond reach and engagement, brands must build:

- Trust
- Sensory memories
- Human connection
- The desire to return

## The Uncomfortable Question for 2027

Are you building experiences people genuinely feel, or simply creating more things for them to ignore?

> Because in a world where consumers feel nothing about your brand, they lose absolutely nothing by leaving.

And that's a terrifying place for any business to be.
