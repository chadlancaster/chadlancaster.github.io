About

Bridging brands & culture through unforgettable storytelling.

Founder

Francine Le Chat

Founder & Chairperson · 1955-2017

A visionary publicist, theatre and television producer, and philanthropist, Fran was integral in building CSA into a cultural force. At a pivotal time in South Africa's history, she brought global icons including Naomi Campbell, Herb Ritts, Djimon Hounsou and Kimora Lee Simmons to the country, creating powerful new connections between South African talent and the world.

She opened CSA's Los Angeles office, expanded the agency's international reach and gave African talent greater visibility, credibility and access on the global stage. Her legacy lives in the careers she shaped, the doors she opened and the belief that African talent belongs at the centre of global culture.

Her name remains at the foundation of CSA. Her standard still sets the bar.
